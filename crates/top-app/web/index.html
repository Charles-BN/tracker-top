Daily survey report

Field survey of pile positions and real ground elevation. CSV, TXT or Excel; with or without a header row (Point, Easting, Northing, Elevation, Code). Points are matched to piles by ID, or by position when the ID is unknown.

Drop survey files here
or click to choose. Several files at once is fine.

Master pile list

The immutable core: pile ID, Easting, Northing, pile type, colour (and tracker, block, section when available). Once loaded it can never be changed; a later file can only add new piles. A ground elevation column (such as Z LIDAR) is also recorded as LIDAR ground.

Drop the master pile list here
or click to choose.

LIDAR / design ground

optional

Ground elevations used for piles that have not been surveyed yet, so whole trackers can be optimised before the survey is complete. A field survey always takes precedence.

Drop a LIDAR file here
or click to choose.

Flood values

not loaded yet

One value per pile, in metres: water depth above ground, or water level (see Settings). Each pile's top is kept above the flood water plus a clearance, which raises the bottom of the reveal window. Pile ID plus a column such as Flood, Flood depth or Flood level. A newer file replaces older values.

Drop a flood file here
or click to choose.
